## Learned
- **SQLite was hiding foreign-key bugs.** It ignores foreign keys unless `PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON` is set, which is why the suite never caught #21. That's now on in `tests/conftest.py`.
- **SQLAlchemy 2.1 treats a bare `postgresql://` URL as `psycopg` (v3), not psycopg2.** Use `postgresql+psycopg2://`, or install `psycopg`. This applies to the consuming apps too.
- Cards don't move to In review on their own unless the PR is linked in the Development sidebar before it merges. Closing the issue still moves the card to Done.
